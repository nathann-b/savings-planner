How this is calculated
  • 2026 federal brackets for a single filer ($16,100 standard deduction), Georgia's flat 4.99% tax ($15,000 standard deduction), and 7.65% Social Security and Medicare.
  • Health insurance is the employee-only Kaiser HMO at $46.55 per paycheck (26 paychecks), taken out pre-tax.
  • The 457(b) is pre-tax and the Roth IRA comes from take-home pay. Contributions go in monthly; returns compound monthly.
  • Salary and contribution limits stay flat (no raises, no limit increases). A CCWA pension contribution, if there is one, isn't included.
  • "In today's dollars" assumes 2.5% yearly inflation. Real returns vary a lot from year to year. This is an estimate, not financial advice.
